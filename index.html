CONTROLS

Click a key, then press the replacement key.

HOW TO PLAY

WASD move • Mouse aim • Click shoot • Space jump

Q wall • C floor • V ramp • R rotate build

1 shotgun • 2 rifle • 3 pickaxe • Esc unlock mouse

Click a placed build while holding the edit key to remove it. Use the build buttons to place structures in front of you.
